Dual subtitles on your Android phone. A free Language Reactor alternative.

Language Reactor is a desktop browser extension, so it doesn’t work in the YouTube app on your phone. DuoSub Study is a separate, free Android app that shows YouTube captions in two languages and replays any sentence with one tap.

Why learners look for an Android alternative

Language Reactor runs in Chrome on desktop and laptop computers. In March 2026 its team said it has no mobile app for Android or iPhone. If you study on your phone, on the bus or before bed, you need something that runs on the phone itself.

DuoSub Study is an independent open-source project. It is not affiliated with Language Reactor and works only with YouTube, not Netflix.

Two subtitle languages at once

The original caption and its translation sit together under the video, or beside it in landscape.

Tap to replay

Tap a subtitle line and the video jumps back to the start of that sentence. No scrubbing.

Look up words

Tap a word for its meaning, part of speech and pronunciation. Japanese words also get a grammar explanation.

Saved words and Anki

Save words with their original sentence, review them with spaced repetition, or export them to Anki.

Real YouTube inside the app

Search, Shorts, comments and recommendations work as usual. Share any YouTube link to the app to open it.

Free, with no account

No subscription, no ads and no sign-up. Translations go into 101 languages, online with Google Translate or on the phone.

Switching from Language Reactor

  1. Download DualSub-Replay.apk on your phone and open it. If Android asks, allow your browser to install unknown apps.
  2. Pick your own language and the language you are learning.
  3. Search YouTube in the app, or share a video from the YouTube app to DuoSub Study.
  4. Watch with both subtitles, tap lines to replay them, and tap words to save them.

Frequently asked questions

Is there a Language Reactor app for Android?

No. Language Reactor is a Chrome extension for desktop and laptop computers, and its team has said there is no mobile app. DuoSub Study is a separate Android app with a similar two-language view for YouTube.

Does DuoSub Study work with Netflix?

No. DuoSub Study works with YouTube videos that have captions, including auto-generated ones.

Can I keep using Language Reactor on my computer?

Yes. The two are unrelated, so you can use Language Reactor on your computer and DuoSub Study on your phone. Saved words don’t sync between them, but DuoSub Study can export to Anki.

Is DuoSub Study free?

Yes. It is free and open source, with no ads, subscriptions or accounts.
